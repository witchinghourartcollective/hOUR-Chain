# Funding and Cloud-Cost Plan

## Objective

Reduce infrastructure burn immediately, finance a working creator-protocol pilot non-dilutively where possible, and raise strategic capital only after the product has evidence.

## Funding ladder

1. Cloud credits: Google for Startups Web3/AI, Microsoft for Startups, and AWS Activate.
2. Base ecosystem funding after a deployed testnet pilot.
3. Filecoin grants for a real decentralized evidence/archive integration.
4. Optimism or Arbitrum funding only when hOUR Chain produces measurable value for those ecosystems.
5. Paid creator pilots for rights setup, provenance, split approvals, settlement receipts, statements, and live activations.
6. Strategic pre-seed financing after pilot traction and a defensible monthly burn model.

## Required funding package

- One-page company and product summary.
- Pitch deck and architecture.
- Working demo and repository.
- 12-month milestone roadmap.
- Monthly infrastructure baseline and forecast.
- Use-of-funds budget.
- Founder/team narrative.
- Entity, cap table, compliance assumptions, and data room.
- Pilot evidence and measurable user outcomes.

## Cost controls

See `CLOUD-COST-BASELINE.md` for the current monthly burn model, provider-level budget thresholds, and the non-production shutdown schedule.

- Tag every resource by product, environment, owner, and cost center.
- Budgets and alerts on every provider.
- Non-production compute scheduled off-hours.
- Remove duplicate databases, nodes, indexers, and AI workloads.
- Separate media processing, AI, chain reads, storage, and settlement costs.
- Review cost per active creator, registered work, and completed settlement monthly.

## Milestones

- A: specification, schemas, cost baseline, deck, and modeled release.
- B: testnet registry, evidence verification, signed splits, and settlement receipt.
- C: three creator pilots and one publishable case study.
- D: grant applications and a focused pre-seed process.

## Rule

Do not use a public token sale as the default way to pay ordinary cloud bills. Token issuance requires separate utility, economic, securities, money-transmission, and disclosure analysis.
