# Funding and Cloud-Cost Plan

## Objective

Reduce verified infrastructure waste immediately, preserve essential services, finance the smallest working creator-protocol pilot non-dilutively where possible, and raise strategic capital after measurable product evidence.

## Current funding position

hOUR Chain is being specified and built by a solo founder, self-funded out of pocket at an owner-estimated **US$485+/month** across cloud infrastructure and AI tooling. The figure is an unreconciled floor based on memory; spend is constrained by personal cash flow rather than technical readiness or lack of a plan.

Cloud credits would directly convert into shipped protocol work by extending infrastructure runway and freeing founder cash for implementation, security, and pilot delivery.

There is currently **no AWS spend**. AWS Activate is a prospective credits program, not a way to reimburse existing AWS infrastructure.

See `CLOUD-COST-BASELINE.md` for the current evidence level, node decision framework, controls, and funding gate.

## What can be cut now

These are operating actions, not fundraising dependencies:

1. Reconcile 90 days of GCP and Azure billing exports.
2. Attribute every charged resource to a workload and owner.
3. Measure the Bitcoin and Ethereum GCP nodes separately.
4. Test whether the Ethereum workload is better served by a capped managed-RPC provider or a resized node.
5. Move Bitcoin/Lightning only through a verified migration and recovery plan; compare against the approximately US$24/month pruned Bitcoin Core + LND VPS profile.
6. Retire, resize, or schedule only resources proven idle or oversized.
7. Activate 75/90/100% budget alerts.
8. Reassess AI subscriptions by shipped value and avoid unsupported blanket cuts.

## What needs funding

Funding requests should map to time-bounded deliverables:

- essential cloud operations and backups;
- a safe node/RPC optimization experiment;
- continuous Lightning infrastructure after migration validation;
- protocol engineering and security work;
- a small creator pilot with measurable settlement and rights workflows;
- evidence storage and verification;
- independent security/legal review where required; and
- founder execution runway.

## Funding ladder

1. **Cloud credits:** Google for Startups Cloud Program, Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub, and AWS Activate.
2. **Infrastructure partners:** RPC, storage, observability, security, and developer-tool credits tied to a defined pilot.
3. **Base ecosystem support:** after a deployed Base testnet workflow and measurable ecosystem contribution.
4. **Filecoin ecosystem support:** only for a real decentralized evidence/archive integration.
5. **Other ecosystem grants:** Optimism, Arbitrum, or similar programs only when hOUR Chain produces measurable value for that ecosystem.
6. **Paid creator pilots:** rights setup, provenance, split approvals, settlement receipts, statements, and live activations.
7. **Strategic pre-seed financing:** after pilot traction, reconciled burn, and defensible unit economics.

## Defensible use-of-funds framing

Do not present the superseded US$2,870/month scenario, invented AWS resources, duplicate Postgres/Redis claims, or US$143.50 per-creator figure.

Use the following structure:

- **Current:** approximately US$485+/month, owner-reported and pending billing reconciliation.
- **Immediate optimization:** determine whether the two GCP full nodes can be resized, replaced, or moved safely.
- **Credits request:** enough capacity to preserve essential infrastructure and ship a measured pilot without diverting scarce founder cash.
- **Scale request:** deferred until 30 days of pilot utilization and unit-cost data exist.

## Evidence gates

Before requesting or approving a new recurring infrastructure commitment:

- identify the exact workload and owner;
- show the current measured utilization or a credible load assumption;
- compare at least one lower-cost architecture;
- define a 30-day deliverable and success metric;
- set a budget and alerts; and
- document the shutdown or migration path.

Before publishing creator economics:

- count monthly active pilot creators;
- separate shared from incremental costs;
- record settlement, RPC, storage, AI, and support costs;
- include founder/support time separately; and
- measure for at least 30 days.

## Rule

Do not use a public token sale as the default way to pay ordinary cloud bills. Token issuance requires separate utility, economic, securities, money-transmission, and disclosure analysis.
